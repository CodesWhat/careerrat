## User Responsibility

You are **solely responsible** for every job application you submit. CareerRat
generates drafts and fills forms on your behalf, and it submits them for you when
they're complete and verified. Review your profile and answers, because CareerRat
submits what they say on your behalf. The tool is not a party to any application and accepts no liability for
its accuracy, completeness, or consequences.

**Submission.** By default CareerRat presses the site's submit control itself,
once, only when every required field is answered, required uploads are attached,
and the application passes its checks. If it can't confirm the submission, it
tells you to check the site. Run `careerrat automation submit manual --write` (or ask the agent to) and it fills
the form and halts so you press the submit control yourself.

## Data Privacy

Your candidate data (profile, evidence, compensation) is stored **locally** on your
machine inside the `.careerrat/candidate/` directory, which is gitignored by default. CareerRat
does not transmit your personal data to any CareerRat-operated server. Data may be
sent to third-party AI provider APIs through the selected local AI runtime in
the course of generating tailored content. Review that provider's privacy
policy before use.
